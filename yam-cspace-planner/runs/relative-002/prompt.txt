Choose one action per reply. Use the measured grasp-point pose and compare
images before and after motion to learn how base-axis movement shifts the jaws
in the image. Judge arrival from the measured motion and camera views.
Make small deliberate corrections when direction or distance is
uncertain. When the views show a clear route, use phase clear_transit and aim
directly for the next hover pose. Each clear-transit call can combine up to
20 cm of translation and 1 rad of rotation. Use this for the approach above
the block and the transfer above X after the block has been lifted clear.
Keep the gripper above the table during transit.

increments as the pads approach the block. Close
when both jaws straddle opposite faces. Lift vertically and confirm the block
