grasp, move_above_x, then place. Use the final hover target for each travel call.
The local planner generates and reviews the intermediate joint motion.

For each motion use the latest MAIN image to identify the target. Give u and v
as fractions from (0,0) at the first pixel center at top-left to (1,1) at the
last pixel center at bottom-right. For the block use the center of its top
face. For X use the center of the printed mark. Use the active arm's wrist
image for jaw alignment and visual confirmation that the block was lifted.
Use the other view if it helps. If the target is occluded, recheck or stop.

should squeeze. Give jaw_line.start and jaw_line.end as two separated
points on the top face, one near each chosen side. The line across those
points is the direction the jaws open and close. The planner keeps the
approach perpendicular to the tabletop. If the active wrist view shows
poor jaw alignment, call move_above_block again with a corrected line
before grasping. Grasp, transfer and place carry the selected angle forward.

another action. If alignment is off, correct the hover. Retry grasp from the
block hover only when the images show the block back on the table and the jaws
empty. Require visible retention before transfer and release. Stop on unclear
contact, invalid feedback, or a clearance rejection. After retracting, check
the block is centered on X before calling done. Report a miss with give_up.
